01 / ABIN DA MASU SARRAFAWA SUKE YI

Zaɓi mai sarrafawa
bisa abin da ake nunawa.

Bambancin hasken rubutu yana duhun shafukan karatu. Inganta bidiyo yana bayyana bayanan hoton da suke duhu. Ƙarfin launi da RGB suna taimaka wa allunan tawadar lantarki masu launi.

Waɗannan samfoti ne na misali. Sakamako ya bambanta bisa allo da asalin abin da ake nunawa.

01 / KARATU

Sa rubutu marar fitowa sosai ya fi duhu

  • Bambancin hasken rubutu yana ba da matakai huɗu don shafuka masu ƙarancin bambancin haske.
  • Yi amfani da shi don karatu; ya bambanta da Inganta bidiyo.

02 / HOTUNA + BIDIYO

Bayyana bayanan da suke duhu

  • Inganta bidiyo yana haskaka bayanan inuwa a hotuna da bidiyo.
  • Kashe shi don karatu domin yana haskaka rubutu mai duhu ma.

03 / TAWADAR LANTARKI MAI LAUNI

Daidaita ƙarfin launi da RGB

  • Yi amfani da saitunan da aka riga aka tsara ko masu sarrafawa kai tsaye don rama ƙarancin faɗin launuka.
  • Samuwa ya dogara da tsarin aiki, GPU, direba, da hanyar fitar da hoto zuwa allo.

04 / GA KOWANE ALLO

Ajiye saituna ga kowane allo

  • Yi wa allunan tawadar lantarki da kake son manhajar ta canza alama kawai.
  • Ajiye lanƙwasoshi don alluna daban-daban kuma maido da saituna lokacin fita daga manhajar.

03 / ƘARIN DAIDAITAWA NA ZAƁI

Daidaita lanƙwasar
idan saitunan da aka riga aka tsara ba su isa ba.

Canza maɓallin lanƙwasawa, gamma, matsayin baƙi, da matsayin fari yayin kallon lanƙwasar.

Ajiye saituna masu suna har guda biyar. Ana ajiye saituna daban ga kowane allo.

macOS 2.6

Shigar a Mac

  1. Sauke kuma buɗe DMG.
  2. Ja E‑Ink Assistant zuwa Manhajoji.
  3. Gwada buɗe manhajar sau ɗaya. Idan macOS ya hana ta, buɗe Saitunan tsarin → Sirri & Tsaro kuma zaɓi Buɗe duk da haka.

Wannan manhaja ce da aka ƙirƙira da kanta kuma ba ta cikin App Store a yanzu. macOS zai nuna gargaɗin “an kasa tabbatarwa” lokacin da ka fara buɗe ta. Lambar gaba ɗaya buɗaɗɗen tushe ce, don haka za ka iya duba ta kafin yanke shawarar amfani da ita.

Idan “Buɗe duk da haka” bai bayyana ba

Bayan ka matsar da manhajar zuwa Manhajoji, buɗe Terminal kuma gudanar da:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/E-Ink Assistant.app"

WINDOWS 1.2

Shigar a Windows

  1. Sauke kuma gudanar da fayil ɗin shigarwa.
  2. Amince da buƙatar mai gudanarwa.
  3. Buɗe E‑Ink Assistant daga menu na Fara ko tiren tsarin.

Don Windows 7 SP1 zuwa Windows 11 a kwamfutocin x64. Masu sarrafawa da ake samu sun dogara da nau'in Windows, GPU, direba, da haɗin allo.
