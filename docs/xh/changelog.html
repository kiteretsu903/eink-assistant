IMBALI YEENGUQULELO

INGUQULELO 2.6

2 EYOMSINTSI 2026

Inkxaso yezikrini ezibonisa umfanekiso omnye iphuculwe.

  • Izikrini zesibini eziphinda umfanekiso ngehardware zisakhethwa ngokwahlukeneyo kwaye zilungiswa ngokuphuma ngakunye kwehardware.
  • Ukutshintsha phakathi kokuphinda umfanekiso nokwandisa kuhlaziya ngokukhawuleza kuze kuphinde kufake iisetingi zesikrini.
  • Ukufaka kwakhona nokubuyisela xa uphuma kuchukumisa kuphela iiphaneli eziphawulwe njenge-e-ink, kuquka nezikrini eziqhagamshelweyo eziphinda umfanekiso.
  • Icebiso lokuseta ngoku libhekisa ngokucacileyo kwimenyu yesikrini ngokwaso.

INGUQULELO 2.5

2 EYOMSINTSI 2026

Kongezwe isikhokelo esifutshane sokuseta ihardware.

  • Isikhumbuzi esifihlekayo ngoku sibonakala ngaphezu kwezilawuli, sinomzekelo weBigme B251 Pro.
  • Ndiyaqonda kufihla isikhumbuzi kwiseshoni; Sukuphinda ubonise kuhlala kunjalo nasemva kokuqalisa kwakhona.
  • Iphaneli yokwamkela ngoku isebenzisa iingongoma ezintathu ezimfutshane zokuseta ezinee-ayikhoni.
  • Izikrini ezakhelwe ngaphakathi ngoku zivela emva kwezikrini zangaphandle kuluhlu lokukhetha.

INGUQULELO 2.4

1 EYOMSINTSI 2026

Ukuphakama kwephaneli kulandela isikrini sangoku.

  • Iphaneli yolawulo ngoku isebenzisa ukuphakama okubonakalayo kwesikrini esinento yebha yemenyu.
  • Umxholo omde uyaskroleka ushiye amanqaku angama40 ngaphezu kweDock okanye komphetho wesikrini. Iiphaneli ezimfutshane zigcina ukuphakama kwazo kwendalo.

INGUQULELO 1.2

EYANGOKU

Ukubuyiselwa kwezilawuli zombala kuphuculwe.

  • Kulungiswe izilumkiso ezingachanekanga zokungaphumeleli kophawu xa iWindows iveza indlela yokusebenza kombala kodwa ingavumeli i-app itshintshe imeko yayo.
  • Idatha yokubuyisela iyagcinwa ngelixa imo yokuphinda umfanekiso icima ulawulo lombala okwethutyana, ize ibuyiselwe xa ulungelelwaniso lwezikrini olulawulekayo lubuya.

INGUQULELO 1.1

Ukukhutshwa kokuqala kweWindows.

  • Kongezwe Umahluko wombhalo, Ukuphucula ividiyo, amagophe ongezelelekileyo, iisetingi ezilungiselelweyo nokukhetha izikrini ze-e-ink kwisikrini ngasinye.
  • Kongezwe izilawuli ezihambelanayo zokutyeba kombala, RGB, imo ekhanyayo yeWindows nesibane sasebusuku, kunye nokubuyisela nokuchacha emva kokuwa kwe-app.
  • Kongezwe ukuphathwa kwezikrini eziphinda umfanekiso, isikhokelo setreyi yenkqubo, ukuqala xa ungena, uhlaziyo nesifakeli esineelwimi ezininzi.
